Wood Care & Mounting Guide

  • ✦ Dusting: Wipe gently with a dry microfiber cloth or soft brush along the carved grain.
  • ✦ Moisture & Sun: Keep away from direct water spray; natural oil finish protects against indoor humidity.
  • ✦ 3D Relief Nourishment: Apply a drop of natural beeswax or mineral oil once a year to deepen walnut richness.
  • ✦ Dual Mounting: Includes both hidden nails and high-strength 3M architectural wall spacers.
